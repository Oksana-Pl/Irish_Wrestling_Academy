Kids Wrestling
Training

Children learn wrestling step by step, starting with movement, balance and simple techniques. Games and partner exercises keep the learning active and enjoyable.

As they progress, they learn how to take an opponent down, defend and stay in control. Training partners are chosen with age, size and experience in mind.

Frequently Asked
Questions

Ask Us a Question
Can I start without experience?

Yes. Beginners are welcome. Tell us you’re new to wrestling and we’ll help you choose a suitable class.

What should I wear and bring?

Bring water and clean sports clothes without zips or exposed metal parts. We’ll confirm footwear and equipment before your visit.

How are children’s groups organised?

Our kids’ programme is for ages 7–15. The coach groups children by age, size and experience for partner practice. A parent or guardian must arrange the first visit and complete registration.

Can parents stay during class?

Yes. Parents can watch from the designated area beside the mats. The coach will explain drop-off and collection arrangements at the first visit.

Do I have to compete?

No. You can train to learn the sport and enjoy the challenge. If you want to compete, your coach will help you plan your next steps.

Can someone aged 16 or 17 join adult training?

Yes, with a parent or guardian’s consent. Speak to the coach first so we can confirm a suitable session.

Can I discuss access or support needs?

Yes. Tell us what would help you take part. We’ll discuss the venue and training arrangements with you before your first visit.

Membership Terms

Payment: membership is paid monthly in advance. There is no automatic renewal or joining fee. A calendar month may contain four or five training weeks.

Pausing or leaving: let the team know before the next month begins. There is no minimum membership term.

Other costs: competition entry, travel, equipment and any required association registration are separate. The team will explain these before you commit.

Changes to classes: members are contacted if a class is cancelled. Any alternative session or credit is agreed with the team.

For parents & guardians

Arrange your child’s first class with the team and let us know their age and experience. We’ll help you choose the right group.

  • Arrive 10 minutes early to meet the coach and complete registration.
  • Bring water and clean sports clothes without zips or exposed metal parts. Ask us about footwear before your visit.
  • Parents can watch from the designated area beside the mats.
  • Let the coach know who will collect your child and speak to us about any support they may need.
